Partner With Us to Lead and Grow a Business

What we're looking for:

We are actively seeking exceptional executives to join us as CEO Partners in our mission to acquire and build enduring, privately held companies. This is a unique opportunity to step into a leadership role as both operator and owner, backed by our capital, experience, and long-term commitment.

We partner with proven leaders—former Presidents, General Managers, or senior executives—who have demonstrated full P&L responsibility and a consistent track record of profitable growth. You’re entrepreneurial, hands-on, and driven to lead a business with purpose and accountability.

Why Partner With Us:

* Own Equity: Participate meaningfully in the value you help create.
* Lead With Autonomy: Run the business your way—with our support.
* Accelerate Growth: Use your operational expertise, and our resources, to drive expansion and efficiency.
* Collaborate on the Acquisition: Help identify and acquire a business that aligns with your strengths and vision.

More than a Job:

This is more than an operating role—it’s a strategic partnership. We are committed to investing in both the business and its leadership for long-term success.

If you're ready to take the next step in your leadership journey and build value as a true partner, we’d love to connect.
